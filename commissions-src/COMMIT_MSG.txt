Stable paging for the Odoo deal lists (Tahiti tracker fluctuation)

Both paginated crm.lead reads in api/odoo.py sorted on a date alone. Many
deals share a date, so each 200-row page cut landed inside a run of ties and
Postgres handed back some records twice and skipped others - which records
changed whenever anything in the CRM was edited. The competition board and
the deals list therefore moved up and down by whole accounts through the day
(Dane Covington: 1.30M true, read as 1.34M and 1.275M on the same day).

The sort now carries id as a tie-breaker, and the merged pages are de-duped
by id as insurance.
